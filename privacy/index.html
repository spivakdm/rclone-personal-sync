Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy describes the account owner's personal Google OAuth application named rclone, used for document synchronization.

Data accessed and purpose

Rclone accesses Google Drive file contents and metadata such as names, paths, identifiers, sizes and modification times to synchronize a selected local folder with a selected Drive folder. Two-way synchronization can create, change and delete files on either side. The Google Drive OAuth permission permits broader Drive access than the selected sync folder.

Storage and processing

Document copies reside on the owner's computer and Google Drive. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the local rclone configuration. Local sync state and logs may contain file paths and operational metadata. This static website does not receive these documents, credentials or tokens.

Sharing and use

Google user data is used only for the owner's requested synchronization. The operator does not sell it, use it for advertising or AI model training, or disclose it to other parties through this application beyond Google as the storage provider. Existing Google Drive sharing settings remain under the account owner's control.

Retention and deletion

Files remain in local or cloud storage until the owner deletes them. Trash, backups or retained versions may keep copies according to their settings. Local logs and credentials remain until removed. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing file copies.

Revoking access

The owner can stop rclone and revoke this application's access in Google Account connections. Local rclone credentials can also be removed. Removing credentials does not delete synchronized documents.

Website hosting

These information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, forms or advertising. GitHub may process visitor connection data under its privacy statement.

Contact

For questions or data requests, use the user support email displayed on the Google authorization screen.
